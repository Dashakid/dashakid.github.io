01Production · GCP

CoKeeper AI

Accountants spend billable hours sorting bank transactions with messy merchant names. CoKeeper runs each one through a five-level vendor cascade and a dual-model classifier, then sorts the results into green, yellow and red confidence tiers, so people only review what the model isn't sure about.

Production accuracy 92.5% · Green tier 99.2% · Review 8h → 30m
